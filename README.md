## 🎮 About the Game

Pizza House Simulator🍕 is a first-person cooking, management, and strategy simulation game developed by Broken Lyre Entertainment and Wendigo Games and published by Devotion Interactive. It released on August 19, 2026, and lets players build and manage a pizzeria on the Amalfi Coast in solo play or online co-op for up to six players.

## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **Pizza House Simulator🍕**. This trainer provides optional quality-of-life enhancements for running the pizzeria, including easier access to money, ingredients, cooking actions, equipment maintenance, customer service, upgrades, and restaurant progression.

**Target Audience:** Players who want to experiment with restaurant layouts, test kitchen automation, explore upgrades, reduce the pressure of busy shifts, or enjoy the game's management systems without normal financial and time constraints.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Movement remains controlled by the game. Use the trainer while navigating the pizzeria, kitchen, and surrounding town, and avoid activating gameplay options while changing scenes or loading into another session.

### Tools & Inventory

Use the ingredient option before preparing orders or restocking kitchen containers. Unlimited ingredients are intended for cooking and service activities and may not immediately update every visual inventory display until the game performs its normal refresh.

### World & Environment

The game features day and night cycles, seasonal weather, a living Italian town, random events, shifting customer behavior, economic changes, and business disasters. Money, upgrade, and protection options are most useful after entering the restaurant-management portion of a session.

### Combat & Weapons

Pizza House Simulator🍕 has no conventional combat system. The trainer focuses on cooking, restaurant management, equipment maintenance, customer service, economy systems, and protection from business-related disruptions rather than combat.
